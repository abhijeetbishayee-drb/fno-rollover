Rollover % = (next + far OI) / (near + next + far OI), from NSE's F&O bhavcopy. DIIF is rollover against the stock's own trailing average. Sector rows are equal-weighted, matching the heatmap board. Click any symbol for its Put-Call Ratio — total PE open interest over total CE open interest across all of that stock's live option contracts. Stock options have no weekly expiry, so unlike the Nifty PCR tracker, WOW and MOM here are the same measure one week and one month apart, not two different expiries priced on the same day. The three PCR columns are shown but are deliberately excluded from SCORE: over 27 windows a cost+PCR short basket returned −1.01pp against cost alone at −1.18pp, so folding PCR in made the one surviving signal worse, not better.
